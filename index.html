WORDLE

How To Play

Guess the WORDLE in 6 tries.

Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. Hit Enter or tap the on-screen ENTER key to submit.

After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

Letters can appear more than once in a word. A letter will only be marked green or yellow once for each time it appears in your guess.

Type on your physical keyboard or use the on-screen keyboard. Press Backspace or tap ⌫ to delete a letter.

A new random word is chosen each time you start a game. Click Play Again! after you win or lose to try another word.

Use the moon/sun button in the header to switch between light and dark mode. Your theme preference is saved automatically.
